# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: curious adults who know little about Africa beyond headlines and want to learn without being talked down to. They arrive without knowing where to start. Other audiences (teachers, people from African countries who can correct or add) are served by the Method and contribution pages, not by the landing.

## Product Purpose
Africa on the Ground is an educational, interactive static site for adults. It gathers history, food, languages, places, capitals, peoples, art and numbers about the 54 African countries, and teaches by doing: guessing before reading, ordering events, sorting data, a quiz, reading trails and games. It is not a travel guide. Success: a visitor leaves having learned something that corrected an assumption, and knows what is documented and what is one person's account.

## Positioning
Two layers are kept apart: the documented record (every claim has a numbered, labelled source; each page states what is not known) and local voices (one person's experience, never presented as fact). The site is static: no database, no cookies, no tracking, nothing saved or sent; scores live only on the page.

## Constraints
- All contributed notes come by hand with the author's consent; the site never collects data.
- Content status is honest: most place, capital, people and art pages are drafts awaiting human review; the landing must not imply completeness.
- Avoid the word "tribe" as a category; use peoples or ethnic groups.
- Fonts are local; no third-party requests.
- Visual identity (palette, fonts, components) is incumbent and is kept; the landing's structure is what changes.

## Landing page brief (confirmed 2026-11-15)
Visitor: an adult with no starting point. Organise the entry by way of engaging: Read (stories, reading trails, capitals, peoples), Look (map, places, art, countries), Play (quiz, society games, connections, which-story game), Contribute (add a voice, Method). Each way shows a live preview of real content. The page should present the project and its method, not dump every item.
